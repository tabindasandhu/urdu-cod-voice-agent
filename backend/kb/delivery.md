# Delivery

## Delivery areas
We deliver to all cities in Pakistan through our courier partners. We do not deliver outside Pakistan.

## Delivery time
Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad and Rawalpindi: 2 to 4 working days after the order is confirmed. Other cities: 4 to 7 working days. Remote areas such as Gilgit-Baltistan, AJK and interior Balochistan: up to 10 working days.

## Delivery charges
The delivery charge is a flat Rs. 250 per order. Delivery is free on orders of Rs. 5,000 or more.

## Order tracking
When the order is dispatched, the customer receives an SMS with a tracking number. The parcel can be tracked on the courier's website using that number.

## Rescheduling delivery
If the customer will not be available, delivery can be postponed once, by up to 3 days, on request during the confirmation call or on WhatsApp.
