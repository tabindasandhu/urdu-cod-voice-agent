# Products

## Unstitched lawn
Three-piece unstitched lawn suits (shirt, dupatta and trouser fabric) priced from Rs. 3,500 to Rs. 6,500.

## Ready-to-wear kurtas
Ready-to-wear kurtas come in sizes XS to XL. Size chart by chest: XS 34 inches, S 36, M 38, L 40, XL 42. Prices range from Rs. 2,800 to Rs. 4,500.

## Formal wear
Embroidered formal suits for weddings and events, priced from Rs. 9,000 to Rs. 22,000. Formal wear is not eligible for size exchange.

## What we do not sell
We do not sell menswear, including men's or gents' kurtas and shalwar kameez. We also do not sell children's clothing, shoes or jewellery, and we do not offer custom stitching.
