# Returns and cancellations

## Return policy
Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery if they are unused, unwashed and have the original tags. Sale items are final and cannot be returned.

## Size exchange
A size exchange is free once per order within 7 days of delivery. The customer sends the item back, and the new size is dispatched after we receive it.

## Refunds
Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 working days after we receive the returned item, by bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. Delivery charges are not refunded.

## Cancelling an order
An order can be cancelled for free before it is dispatched. Customers who repeatedly refuse COD parcels at the door may lose the COD option on future orders.
