# Zari Collective

## About
Zari Collective is an online women's clothing brand based in Lahore, Pakistan, founded in 2021. We sell unstitched lawn suits, ready-to-wear kurtas and formal wear. Orders are placed on our website and Instagram page.

## Working hours
Customer support is available Monday to Saturday, 10am to 7pm. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays. Orders placed outside these hours are processed on the next working day.

## Contact
Customers can reach support on WhatsApp at 0300-0000000 or by email at support@zari-example.pk. Complaints about riders or damaged parcels should be sent on WhatsApp with the order number and a photo.
