# Payment

## Cash on delivery
Cash on delivery (COD) is available for orders up to Rs. 50,000. The customer pays the rider in cash. There is no extra fee for COD.

## Why we confirm COD orders
We call every COD customer to confirm the order before dispatch, to avoid fake orders and wrong addresses. If an order is not confirmed within 24 hours, it is cancelled automatically.

## Online payment
Customers can also pay in advance by bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. Advance payment is optional for COD orders.

## Opening the parcel
Customers may open the parcel in front of the rider to check the items before paying. If an item is wrong or damaged, the customer can refuse the parcel and does not have to pay.
